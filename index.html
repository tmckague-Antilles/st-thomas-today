Best beach today

Lindbergh Bay

It sits on the west end, out of the east wind and the north swell, and it met standards in the latest DPNR test. Four ships are in port, so skip the excursion beaches (Magens, Coki, Sapphire) and enjoy the quieter side.

Rip currents Moderate today Surf around 4 ft UV index 8, very high (latest) Wind East, 10 to 12 mph

Also passedEvery St. Thomas beach tested met standards for Nov 16 to 20: Secret Harbor, Bolongo Bay, Lindqvist, Brewers Bay, Limetree, Frenchman's Bay, Magens, Bluebeards, Hull Bay, Sapphire, Coki Point and Vessup Bay. Honeymoon Beach on Water Island passed too. Nothing to skip this week.

Not sampledWater Bay and the St. John beaches were not tested, so their water quality is unknown.

Surf and windSurf stays near 4 feet through Tuesday with a moderate rip current risk, mostly on north-facing beaches, and a new north swell arrives Sunday. Surf eases to about 3 feet Wednesday. East wind is about 10 mph through Sunday, then 15 mph.
