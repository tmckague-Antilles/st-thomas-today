Heads up

Big northwest swell Monday night to WednesdayRip currents are low today but turn moderate Monday. A long-period swell arrives Monday evening and peaks Tuesday, with surf near 5 feet here and stronger rips on north-facing beaches. Boaters, take note: seas of 5 to 7 feet in open waters.

Very strong sunUV index 10 in the latest EPA reading, which is very high. Sunscreen, a hat and shade from late morning to mid-afternoon.

Wetter weather late in the weekShowers are likely most days. From Thursday the pattern turns wetter, with a chance of ponding and flooding on roads. Heat index may top 100° later in the week.

Four ships Tuesday and four WednesdayTuesday and Wednesday are the packed days downtown, with over 16,000 passengers each. Monday and Thursday have no ships.
