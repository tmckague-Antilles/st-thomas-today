Heads up

High rip current risk through this afternoonSurf is around 6 feet today with life-threatening rip currents on north-facing beaches. Risk drops to moderate Thursday (surf about 4 feet), then rough seas return Friday into the weekend.

Wetter Thursday to SaturdayTropical moisture arrives tonight. Expect scattered to numerous showers and thunderstorms Thursday through Saturday, with lightning, gusty winds and heavy downpours. Road ponding and flooding in low spots are possible. Drier by early next week.

Exceptional drought on St. ThomasThe latest drought update (Oct 8) puts St. Thomas in the worst category, D4. Keep conserving water even with rain in the forecast.

Very strong sun and warmUV index 10 in the latest EPA reading, which is very high. Today is warm, so drink water and take shade breaks. Another warm spell is possible Sunday into Monday.

Four ships in port todayA packed day downtown, with over 18,000 passengers. Thursday has no ships.

Tropics are quiet for usThe Hurricane Center is watching a storm-force non-tropical low off the US East Coast, with a 40% chance of becoming subtropical in 7 days. It is expected to move away to the northeast. Nothing is expected near the Virgin Islands.
